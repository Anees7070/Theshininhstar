For Admission

Enrolling in our school is an excellent decision for your children's future. Here, along with education, special attention is given to character building, where students are provided not only with academic knowledge but also opportunities to develop their personalities. Experienced teachers, modern teaching methods, and a peaceful learning environment help students grow and succeed. Our goal is to make every student a responsible and successful individual.

For Applying Teacher

We are looking for a teacher. The candidate should have at least a relevant degree in the subject along with teaching experience. They should possess strong communication skills, be able to explain concepts in a simple and effective way, and maintain discipline and professionalism. Familiarity with modern teaching methods, as well as an interest in guiding and nurturing students, is essential to help create an excellent learning environment.

For Applying Peon

We are looking for a peon. The candidate should be responsible and hardworking. They should have basic education and be able to perform daily school tasks such as cleaning, delivering documents, and assisting with small duties efficiently. Punctuality, honesty, and a good attitude towards staff and students are also very important to maintain an organized and peaceful school environment.

About School

The Shining Star is committed to providing quality education and holistic development to every student. Established with a vision to empower young girls through education and character building.
